# Glossary

Terms for the paper Vintage Cube and the tool that maintains it. Resolved with the Owner on 2026-10-24.

## Cube
The physical, paper cube: a box of about 540 proxy cards that the group drafts with. The Cube is
the thing being maintained; everything else describes or serves it.

## List
The intended contents of the Cube, kept on CubeCobra (`MTGVintageCube`). The List says which card
names should be in the Cube. The Cube is verified against the List.

## Upstream
The MTGO Vintage Cube list as published on CubeCobra (`modovintage`). The List is derived from
Upstream by applying the group's Winner's Picks and Bans.

## Winner's Pick
A card chosen by the winner of a draft night. It enters the List and stays there permanently,
even if Upstream later cuts it. Recorded with the winner's name and the date.

## Ban
A card the group has removed from the List for good. A Ban is sticky: if Upstream adds the card
again, it does not re-enter the List.

## Update
One batch of changes to the List: the cards coming In and the cards going Out, usually triggered
by an Upstream change or a Winner's Pick.

## Printing
One specific published version of a card, identified by set code and collector number. Two
Printings of the same card differ in art, frame or wording.

## Proxy
A printed stand-in for a card. Every card in the Cube is a Proxy, so any Printing can be
produced on demand and replacing a card is cheap.

## Print run
A set, frame or edition chosen so that every card in a Cycle shares the same look.

## Cycle
A group of cards that belong together and should share one Print run: the fetchlands, the
original dual lands, the Moxen, the Talismans, and similar groups.

## Print batch
The set of Printings sent to the proxy printer in one go, typically the In cards of an Update
plus any replacements.

## Art flag
A note that a card's current Printing should be replaced, with the reason and who raised it.
It stays on the card until a new Printing is in the Cube.

## Scan
A pass over the physical Cube with a phone camera, in any order, that records which cards and
Printings were seen. A Scan ends with a diff against the List: missing and unexpected cards.

## Family
Synonym for Cycle, as the group says it: "the talismans are a family". The glossary uses Cycle.

## Suggestion
A proposed change to the List, the Cube record or a Printing, made by anyone who has the link.
A Suggestion does nothing until the Owner confirms it.

## Owner
The one person who confirms Suggestions and whose confirmation turns them into
Updates, Print batches or record changes.

## Print sheet
The file sent to a printer for one Print batch, laid out so that each card comes out at the
intended physical size without changing printer settings.

## Calibration
Measuring a printed card against the intended size and recording the correction so that later
Print sheets match it.

## In-box Printing
The Printing that was actually printed and is in the Cube. The authority on what the Cube looks like.

## Wanted Printing
The Printing the Owner would like a card to have. When it differs from the In-box Printing, the
difference is a reprint in the next Print batch.
